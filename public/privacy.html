PadelTOP Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

PadelTOP ("the app") is a padel tournament manager. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. We keep this simple because the app itself is simple.

Information we collect

The only information the app stores is the data you enter to run tournaments:

  • Player names you add to the player registry.
  • Tournament data — tournament names, match schedules, scores, and results.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, or any device analytics. There are no user accounts and no login.

How the information is used

The data you enter is used solely to provide the app's features: generating match schedules, tracking scores, showing leaderboards, and keeping a history of tournaments and player statistics. It is used for no other purpose.

Where the information is stored

To let you access your tournaments across devices, the player and tournament data you enter is stored on a private hosted database operated on our behalf (Render). It is transmitted over an encrypted connection. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. The only processor involved is our hosting provider, which stores the data so the app can function.

Data retention and deletion

Tournament and player data remains available until it is deleted from within the app. You can remove players and delete tournaments at any time using the app's controls. To request deletion of all data, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
